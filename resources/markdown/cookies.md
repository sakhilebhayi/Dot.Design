This page explains the cookies Dot.Design actually sets. There aren't many, Dot.Design doesn't run advertising or analytics trackers, so every cookie below exists to make the platform work, not to profile you or serve you ads.

## Cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `dotdesign-session` | Keeps you signed in and remembers your session state as you move around Dot.Design. Strictly necessary. | Ends when your session expires (2 hours without activity) or you sign out. |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | A security token that protects forms and requests on Dot.Design from being forged by another site. Strictly necessary. | Ends when your session expires or you sign out. |
| `remember_web_...` | Only set if you tick "Remember me" when signing in. Keeps you signed in on that browser after you close it. | Up to 5 years, or until you sign out or clear it. |

## Settings kept in your browser

Dot.Design also keeps a few things in your browser's own storage (not cookies, and never sent to us automatically). They only make the editor work the way you left it:

| Stored in your browser | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Colour theme, list or grid view, the last panel you used, your measurement unit, the colours you saved | Remember your choices on this device. |
| A crash-recovery copy of a page with changes that were not saved yet (`dotdesign:studio:journal:...`) | Lets the editor offer your last changes back after a crash or a lost connection. It is removed once the page is saved, and anything older than 30 days is removed when you next open that design. On a shared computer, save your work and clear site data when you leave. |

Share-link viewers (people who open a design through a link without an account) get no cookie at all. The only thing kept in their browser is their light or dark theme choice.

## What we don't use

Dot.Design does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or tracking pixels, or cross-site marketing cookies.

## Cookies set by connected platforms

If you use single sign-on to move between Dot.Design and another Dot Ecosystem platform, that platform may set its own cookies once you land on it, those are covered by that platform's own Cookie Policy, not this one.

## Managing cookies

Because Dot.Design's cookies are strictly necessary, turning them off in your browser will likely break sign-in and core functionality. Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings if you'd still like to manage them yourself.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the cookies Dot.Design sets change. We'll change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
